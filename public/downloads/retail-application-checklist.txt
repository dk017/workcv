WorkCV - Retail application checklist

1. Use the exact shop role and employer from the advert.
2. Name only tills, payment systems and product areas you have used.
3. Give a specific customer-help example you can explain.
4. Separate volunteering from paid employment.
5. State realistic evening, weekend and holiday availability.
6. Check dates and responsibilities agree in the CV and letter.

Example and personalisation: https://workcv.co.uk/cv-template-retail-uk
