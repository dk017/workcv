Customer service application checklist — WorkCV

1. Match the role title and employer to the advert.
2. Name only channels and systems you have actually used.
3. Describe a complaint you can explain in an interview.
4. Check that CV dates and letter claims agree.
5. Use numbers only when you can substantiate them.
6. Follow the employer's requested PDF or Word format.
