Warehouse application checklist — WorkCV

1. Match the role to picking, packing, goods-in or dispatch as appropriate.
2. Distinguish equipment awareness from training and authorised use.
3. Check that shift availability and travel arrangements are realistic.
4. Give an accurate example of checking an order or spotting a discrepancy.
5. Keep dates, responsibilities and training consistent in both documents.
6. Use the employer's requested file format and filename.
