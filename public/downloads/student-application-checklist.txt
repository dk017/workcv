WorkCV - Student and first-job application checklist

1. Put current education and its expected completion date in the CV.
2. Use projects, volunteering or societies when you have no paid work.
3. Label unpaid responsibilities and supervised tasks accurately.
4. Do not invent predicted grades, qualifications or job titles.
5. Check work availability against study and travel commitments.
6. Use the same facts in the CV and letter, following the advert instructions.
