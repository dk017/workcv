Care worker application checklist — WorkCV

1. Distinguish paid care work, volunteering and unpaid family care.
2. List only training completed or clearly marked in progress.
3. Keep client names and private health details out of your examples.
4. Describe your own duties without claiming clinical responsibilities you did not hold.
5. Check the employer's shift and travel requirements against your actual availability.
6. Make sure dates, training and responsibilities agree across the CV and letter.

Free checklist. Use your own facts; examples are fictional.
