## Publication quality gate

A trend-led page is indexable only when all answers are yes:

1. Does the first screen answer the query rather than describe what the page will discuss?
2. Is there a concrete asset: comparison, calculator, worksheet, template, example or working tool?
3. Can a visitor complete the task without buying anything?
4. Are changing facts linked to current first-party or authoritative sources and dated?
5. Are rankings, outcomes, vacancy counts, endorsements and expertise claims verifiable?
6. Is the WorkCV money-page link a logical next step after value has been delivered?
7. Is the content meaningfully different from the other pages in the cluster?
8. Is there a refresh or retirement trigger for volatile topics?

## Conversion path

The cluster uses an earned transition rather than an interruptive sales pitch:

`search intent → useful answer/tool → visitor has a vacancy or evidence → tailor and preview a CV → no-subscription money page → editor`

Contextual links should explain why the CV is the next step. Examples: “Found a suitable vacancy? Build the CV for that vacancy” and “Keep the follow-up consistent with the application they reviewed.” Avoid dropping an unrelated “build a CV” banner before the page has answered the query.
