## Interpretation of the supplied export

The export shows strong commercial head terms such as “free resume builder”, “resume builder online”, “ai resume builder” and branded builders. WorkCV should not create thin pages for each competitor or synonym. The better opportunity is to answer the narrower jobs the leaders leave implicit:

- “free CV template UK download” — direct, honest Word file with a clear free boundary;
- “how to edit a CV template in Word UK” — practical editing and PDF-export instructions;
- “CV builder no subscription UK” and “one off CV builder UK” — transparent build-before-pay explanation;
- role plus “template”, “example” and “personal statement” combinations — specific evidence rather than generic claims;
- “career change CV example UK” and “return to work CV example UK” — situation-led guidance with honest timelines.

## Editorial guardrails

- Do not call the paid PDF flow “free”. Say “free to build” only where the page also explains the one-time download price.
- Do not promise ATS compatibility, interviews, recruiter approval, NHS/NMC approval or guaranteed rankings.
- Keep examples fictional or clearly labelled as examples; never imply a testimonial or outcome without evidence.
- Keep the free DOCX route direct and visible. Do not add an email gate, account requirement or hidden payment step.
- Keep role pages useful when the reader does not buy: give structure, tailored examples, source notes and a relevant next step.

## Measurement plan

Track Search Console impressions, clicks, CTR and average position separately for the free-template, Word-guide, no-subscription, pricing, role-example and situation clusters. In analytics, distinguish direct DOCX downloads from editor starts and paid PDF downloads. Review queries after 28 days, then expand only where impressions and engagement show a real unanswered intent.
