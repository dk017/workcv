## Conclusions

1. **Traffic lives in free templates, CV examples and cover-letter examples, all with low KD.** standout-cv shows the model: a large library of useful free templates and examples, each linking to a builder. WorkCV has the pages but ranks on pages 5–8.
2. **The best-value cluster is "cover letter example uk" (6,600, KD 9) plus role-specific letter examples (KD 0).** These pair directly with the new CV + matching cover letter purchase.
3. **Redundancy (≈90k combined volume, KD 15–17)** is the largest single pool, and it reaches the highest-value customer type. WorkCV already has the calculator; ranking it is a matter of quality and links.
4. **Buyer-intent "CV builder" terms are expensive (CPC ~£7–8) and harder (KD 30–43).** Don't plan on paid search at current prices.
5. **The Job Search Pass has little direct search demand.** Its sales will come from on-site offers (post-download, checkout), not SEO.

## Suggested SEO priorities

1. **UK CV examples and templates hub:** upgrade `/cv-examples-uk`, `/professional-cv-template-uk` and `/tools/blank-cv-template-uk` into a best-in-class set of free Word templates and UK examples ("uk cv example" 5,400/KD 13; "cv template word uk" 12,100/KD 26; "student cv example" 2,400/KD 13).
2. **Cover letter examples UK plus role letters** (6,600/KD 9; internship, law, engineering, student, retail, teacher, finance, customer service, apprentice at KD 0–4), each paired with a matching CV example and "make both in WorkCV".
3. **Redundancy calculator:** improve and earn links (49,500/KD 17 head term), with the new tracked CV button.
4. **Quick answers:** "how long should a cv be" (2,900/KD 0), "personal statement cv" (2,400/KD 6), "cv checker" (2,900/KD 7), "ats cv template" (720/KD 0), "career change at 50" (1,000/KD 0).
5. **Role CV pages** (KD 0, 170–590 each) as matched CV + cover letter pairs. Quality over quantity; don't mass-produce thin pages.
