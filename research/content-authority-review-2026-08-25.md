## Editorial decisions

- The page contains 12 fictional examples. Numbers and outcomes are teaching prompts, not claims about real WorkCV users.
- The NHS walkthrough uses a real public advert but a fictional candidate. It quotes/paraphrases only the small set of criteria necessary to explain the method.
- The page uses the truthful organisation byline `WorkCV Editorial Team`. No named author or reviewer was supplied with verifiable identity, role, consent and bio/URL before implementation.
- No testimonials, ratings, interview claims or verified user outcomes were added.
- The page explains that a Civil Service application personal statement is a separate, usually longer response governed by the vacancy's Success Profiles criteria and word limit.
