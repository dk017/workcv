- The volumes for graduate terms need checking (see "Decisions needed").

## Why we rank low

1. **Wrong format for the search.** Ranking pages (CV Genius, Resume.io, CVMaker, Indeed, Reed; MyPerfectCV and Careers Wales for career change) show **several examples** (by degree, or by career move), each with a template and a how-to. We show **one** example on each page, framed as a template.
2. **Missing subtopics** that those pages and "People also ask" cover:
   - **Graduates:**
     - how to list your grade or a predicted 2:1;
     - A-levels and UCAS points;
     - modules and the dissertation;
     - placement years;
     - CVs with no experience;
     - length;
     - **graduate scheme applications**.
   - **Career change:**
     - which CV format to use (chronological or hybrid);
     - personal statement examples;
     - how to explain the change;
     - retraining and courses.
3. **Titles and H1s don't use the searched phrases.** For example, the graduate H1 is "Build a graduate CV that proves more than your degree title", with no "graduate CV example". The career change H1 is "Change career without making your CV look junior".
4. **Little internal support.** Few pages link in with descriptive anchors. Related pages (personal statements, cover letter examples, the student CV, the transferable-skills tool) aren't tied together.
5. **Lower authority than Indeed, Prospects and Reed.** Better pages can realistically reach page 1–2 for the long-tail ("graduate cv example uk", "career change cv examples uk"). Head terms ("graduate cv", "career change cv") will also need links (see outreach).

## Graduate page plan (~2 days)

Keep the URL (it already has history). Retarget it as **the UK graduate CV examples page**.

- **Title:** "Graduate CV Examples UK (2026): Template + Examples by Degree".
- **H1:** "Graduate CV examples and template for UK jobs".
- **Meta:** mention examples by degree, graduate schemes and the free template.
- **5–6 editable examples by degree,** each as a new role template that opens in the editor ("Edit this example"), with "why it works" notes:
  1. Business / Economics → analyst (existing)
  2. Computer Science → junior software developer / IT
  3. Engineering → graduate engineer
  4. Psychology / Social Sciences → HR, research or public sector
  5. English / Humanities → marketing or communications
  6. Biology / Science → lab, healthcare or science roles
- **Graduate schemes section:**
  - Employers received **140 applications per graduate vacancy** on average (ISE, last two years; verified on ise.org.uk). So tailor each application.
  - Link the job tracker and Job Application Pack.
  - Use a graduate version of the Pass offer: "Applying to several graduate schemes?"
  - Any claim about application timing (for example, when schemes open and close) must be checked against a source before it goes in.
- **How-to, kept short:**
  - section order for graduates (education near the top);
  - how to show a grade, a predicted grade, A-levels or UCAS points;
  - modules and the dissertation;
  - placement year, part-time jobs, societies;
  - length;
  - UK personal details: no photo, date of birth, age or nationality, per the National Careers Service (already verified).
- **FAQ (schema)** from "People also ask":
  - How long should a graduate CV be?
  - Should I include my A-levels?
  - How do I show a predicted grade?
  - What if I have no work experience?
  - Do I need a different CV for each graduate scheme?
- **Links:**
  - the student CV and first-job pages;
  - the internship cover letter example;
  - graduate personal statement examples;
  - the ATS CV checker and job tracker;
  - the right-to-work CV page (international students on the Graduate visa).

## Career change page plan (~1.5 days)

- **Title:** "Career Change CV Examples UK + Template (How to Write One)".
- **H1:** "Career change CV examples for UK jobs".
- **4 editable career-move examples**, each with before/after notes on how old duties map to the new role:
  1. Teacher → learning and development / training
  2. Retail or hospitality manager → operations / office
  3. Hospitality or retail → customer service / contact centre
  4. Trades or construction → project coordination
- **Which format to use:** chronological versus a hybrid with a skills summary at the top, with honest guidance on when each works.
- **6 career change personal statement examples** (targets "career change cv personal statement examples", now at 60).
- **Explaining the change:** one CV line, one cover-letter paragraph, one interview answer. Link the career change cover letter if one exists; otherwise add one to the cover letter examples.
- **Retraining:** sourced links only (National Careers Service, GOV.UK Skills Bootcamps), checked before publishing.
- **Tools:** the transferable-skills translator, the Job Application Pack, and a career-change version of the Pass offer ("Applying for different kinds of roles? Make a CV for each").
- **FAQ (schema):** How do I write a CV for a career change? Should I hide my old career? Which CV format is best? How do I explain a career change?
