## Explicit exclusions

- AlternativeTo is not a prospect for this product: its FAQ lists resume/CV builders among categories it generally declines, prohibits website links in descriptions, and warns against promotional profiles. Do not submit WorkCV there.
- MoneySavingExpert and The Student Room are not suitable for promotional links because their community rules restrict commercial promotion.
- Do not use university careers-service support channels as a marketing list. A university approach must be permission-first and should lead with a free, non-commercial resource (for example an ATS checklist), not the paid builder.
