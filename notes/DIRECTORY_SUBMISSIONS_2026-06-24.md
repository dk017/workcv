## UK local-directory note

Directories such as Yell, Thomson Local, Scoot, FreeIndex, and other UK business directories usually expect a trading address, phone number, local service area, and business-owner verification. WorkCV is an online product rather than a local service business, so these are lower priority unless there is a public business address and phone number to list consistently.

If local UK citations are still desired, create a separate NAP record first:
- Legal/trading name
- Public phone number
- Public business address or service-area policy
- Company registration details, if applicable
- Business hours

Do not submit inconsistent address/phone details just to get listings.
