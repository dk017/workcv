week, a different note for each, lead with a free resource, never pay for links, stop if an organisation does
not take resource suggestions. No one on this list has been contacted.

cost £1.95–£2.95, then renew every four weeks at £16.95–£20.95. Three months costs £53.80 at MyPerfectCV,
£61.50 at LiveCareer and £49.95 at Resume.io (quarterly plan). Always say WorkCV sells a competing product.
