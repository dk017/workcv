Send no more than five genuinely relevant approaches per week, explain the audience benefit, and stop if the organisation does not invite resource suggestions. Never buy links, automate submissions, or reuse an identical pitch.

## Operating notes

- Prioritise Prospects, Skills for Care, and Career Returners because the audience-resource fit and public editorial/contact routes are clearest.
- Approach university services only with a free resource first; do not lead with the paid builder.
- Treat National Careers Service, NHS Health Careers, and Citizens Advice as permission-first prospects. Their support channels must not be used for unsolicited marketing.
- Record a mention only after checking that it is live, relevant, editorially legitimate, and not paid or undisclosed.
